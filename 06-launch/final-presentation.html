Roamly Groups

Win the organizer. Remove the coordination tax. Let one successful booking acquire the group.

Product School GTM Certification
1 · GTM Strategy

Start with the organizer, not all group travel.

Beachhead

Self-organized friend groups of 3-8 travelers. Narrow enough to target, large enough to prove the loop.

Problem

Groups want one experience, but the current flow forces separate bookings, external payment tools, and one person chasing everyone.

Motion

Product-led growth. One organizer starts the group, invites friends, and brings new travelers into Roamly.

Organizer→Group created→Friends join→Booking completes
2 · Competitive Intelligence

Airbnb owns trip context. Roamly can own the commitment moment.

Primary competitor

Airbnb Experiences has brand trust, trip context, and a broad travel ecosystem. Roamly should not fight that battle directly.

Wedge

Who has to put the whole group experience on their card? That question exposes the difference between planning together and committing together.

The strategic bet: the remaining pain is not discovery. It is getting several people onto one reservation without making one organizer carry the money risk.

3 · Positioning

From interest to booked and paid.

For the friend who ends up organizing the group's activities and needs everyone to commit and pay without chasing the group or fronting the cost, Roamly Groups is a shared booking experience for curated local activities.

Category

Shared booking experience for curated local activities.

Alternative

Group chats, separate bookings, payment apps, and one person coordinating it all.

Differentiator

Each traveler joins, commits, and pays their share inside one coordinated reservation.

4 · Messaging

Roamly Groups turns group interest into shared commitment.

Organizer: Stop carrying the group Traveler: Your spot, your share Host: One group, one booking

From "we should do this" to booked.

One shared booking for your group. Everyone joins and pays their own share.

5 · Pricing

Do not charge for the behavior we need to create.

Keep Groups free to use. Earn more only when the group successfully books.

Model

Transaction-based through existing Roamly marketplace economics.

Package

No organizer fee, participant fee, or subscription in V1.

Validation

Use conjoint research and beta economics to test whether added conversion beats added operational cost.

6 · Launch and Insights

Scale only after the riskiest assumption survives contact with users.

Pre-launch

Instrument the funnel, recruit high-intent organizers, validate host capacity, and establish a control baseline.

Launch

Release to a controlled slice. Watch invite acceptance, payment completion, GMV, contribution margin, and support burden.

Post-launch

Scale only where conversion and economics improve without operational drag.

Biggest aha: GTM gets stronger every time you remove something. Narrow the audience, sharpen the bet, and test the assumption that can break the strategy.

Closing

One organizer. One shared booking. Multiple future customers.

Roamly Groups wins if it turns a conversion leak into a product-led acquisition loop.
